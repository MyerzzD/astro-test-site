Agility isn't just for collies. Any fit, healthy dog over 12 months can have a go, and most absolutely love it. We build up gradually from low jumps and straight tunnels to full mini-courses over five weeks.

Runs on the paddock in summer and inside the barn in winter. Bring wellies.
