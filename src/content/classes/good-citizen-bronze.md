A structured, seven-week obedience course following the Kennel Club Good Citizen Dog Scheme. Ideal for dogs over a year old who know the basics and are ready to polish them.

Assessment takes place in the final week and successful dogs receive their **Bronze certificate and rosette**. Silver and Gold courses follow on.
