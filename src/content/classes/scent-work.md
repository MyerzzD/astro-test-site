Ten minutes of sniffing tires a dog out more than an hour of fetch. Scentventure teaches your dog to search for a target scent in ever-trickier hiding places, indoors and out.

Brilliant for **older dogs, nervous dogs and dogs recovering from injury** because it's low-impact and builds confidence.
