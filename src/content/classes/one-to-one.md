Some things need a tailored plan rather than a group class. A 1-2-1 starts with a chat about what's happening, then a practical session, and you go home with a **written step-by-step plan** plus two weeks of email support.

Common reasons people book: barking and lunging at dogs on walks, panicking when left alone, guarding food or toys, fear of visitors, and general "we just don't know where to start".
