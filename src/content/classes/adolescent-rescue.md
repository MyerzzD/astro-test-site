Somewhere around six months your perfect puppy turns into a teenager who has forgotten their name. It's normal, it's temporary, and this course gets you through it with your sanity intact.

We focus on the three things owners struggle with most: **recall around distractions**, **walking without pulling**, and **calm behaviour** when the doorbell goes or another dog appears.
