Six weeks of gentle, play-based learning for you and your new best friend. Each week we cover a mix of **life skills** (settling, handling, being left alone) and **foundation cues** (sit, down, come, loose lead), with time for supervised puppy play so your pup learns good doggy manners.

### You'll leave with
- A puppy who comes when called (most of the time!)
- Toilet-training and crate-training plans that actually work
- Confidence handling paws, ears and teeth for vet and groomer visits
- A WhatsApp group of local puppy pals for walks
