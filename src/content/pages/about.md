Fenland Paws was started in 2014 in a draughty barn with three dogs and a bag of cheese. We've since trained well over a thousand dogs from across Wisbech, March, Downham Market, King's Lynn and the surrounding villages.

We believe training should be **fun for the dog and fun for you**. If you're not laughing at least once a session, we're doing it wrong.

### Our promise
- Reward-based, force-free methods — always
- Small classes, never more than six dogs
- Honest advice, even if that means telling you a class isn't right for your dog yet
- Every course includes notes, videos and support between sessions
